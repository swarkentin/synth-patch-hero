Use headphones. Works on desktop and phones.

USB / MIDI keyboards work in Chrome, Edge, Opera and Firefox on a computer, and Chrome on Android (with a USB-C OTG adapter). Plug the keyboard in, then tap Connect.

iPhone and iPad: Safari can't talk to MIDI keyboards, so use the on-screen keys above. Slide your finger across them to glide.

Short keyboard? Use its octave buttons to reach low notes like A1. Middle C (C4) is marked with a dot.
